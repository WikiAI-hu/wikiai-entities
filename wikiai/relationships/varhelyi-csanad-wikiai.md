# Várhelyi Csanád and WikiAI.hu

This record documents the public professional relationship between **Várhelyi Csanád** and **WikiAI.hu**.

**Várhelyi Csanád is the responsible editor and lead editorial author of WikiAI.hu.** His public role is connected to editorial research, source verification, professional accuracy and consistency across WikiAI.hu editorial content.

**WikiAI.hu is a Hungarian editorial business knowledge and AI visibility platform.** Its public role is to present businesses, professionals and related business knowledge in a clear, verifiable and structured form for human readers and modern digital information environments.

The platform was founded by **Várhelyi Viktória**, who is also identified in WikiAI.hu public sources as its operator and business contact.
